# Pitch — The Baniya Syn

**The Baniya Syn is a creative-network workspace that helps brands move from finding AI-native talent to shaping a campaign brief.** It brings creator discovery, shortlists, campaign tracking, brief drafting, and creative inspiration into one clear experience.

## Why it matters
Teams exploring AI-assisted creative production need ways to evaluate creative practice, explain workflows, set expectations, and clarify usage rights.

## Prototype highlights
- Searchable creator cards with specialties and tool stacks
- Campaign pipeline and delivery statuses
- Guided brief form for objectives, mood, budget, timing, and rights
- Clear demo-data disclaimers

## Roadmap
- Verified creator profiles and portfolios
- Creator submissions and availability management
- Persistent briefs, shortlists, and campaign records
- Real AI brief service with disclosure
- Verified trend data and analytics
