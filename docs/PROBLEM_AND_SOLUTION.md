# Problem and solution — The Baniya Syn

## Problem
Brands and creative teams can struggle to discover AI-native creators, compare specialties and workflows, and turn early campaign ideas into briefs with clear deliverables and usage expectations.

## Solution
The Baniya Syn brings creator discovery, campaign tracking, brief drafting, and creative inspiration into one workspace.

## Core experience
1. Browse sample creator profiles and filter by specialty or tool.
2. Save profiles to a shortlist.
3. Review campaign statuses and delivery timelines.
4. Generate a structured creative brief from campaign inputs.
5. Explore illustrative creative signals for inspiration.

## Current scope and limitations
Profiles and metrics are illustrative. Brief generation is template-based and local; trend cards are inspiration, not live measurements. Confirm creator identity, availability, rates, and commercial rights independently.
